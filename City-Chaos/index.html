CITY CHAOS 3D

HOW TO PLAY

Left side: move / drive (up = gas, down = brake and reverse).

Right side: drag to turn the camera when on foot.

ENTER / EXIT: get in a car or out of it. You can steal traffic cars.

FIRE: shoot on foot (auto aim). In a car it is the horn.

Reach the yellow beam to get paid. Pick up cash and hearts.

Crimes give you stars and the police chase you. If they catch you, you go to prison: pay the fine and wait, or bribe the guard.
